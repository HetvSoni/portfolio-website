Supply Chain Documentation Automation Pipeline
Hologic Inc. · Supply Chain & AI Automation

End-to-end document intelligence pipeline monitors supply chain directories, detects incoming image-based process docs, and converts them into structured editable Draw.io flowcharts automatically. From the moment an image lands in a folder, the pipeline runs without any human intervention.

Python Watchdog Computer Vision OCR Draw.io XML DAG Construction Process Automation Supply Chain
Pipeline 4 stages
01
Filesystem Watchdog & Event Ingestion
Python's watchdog monitors designated folders fires a trigger on file-create events for PNG/JPG/TIFF with zero manual kick-off.
02
Vision-Based Content Extraction & OCR
Computer vision + OCR decompose raw process images into shapes, swimlanes, decision branches, connectors, and embedded text.
03
DAG Construction & XML Serialisation
Node-edge topology reconstructed as a directed acyclic graph and serialised into Draw.io-compliant .drawio XML with layout and hierarchy preserved.
04
Auto-Export & Output Delivery
Generated .drawio files deposited into mapped output directories immediately editable and ready for SOP and regulatory workflows.
